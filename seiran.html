SEIRAN HOSPITAL // LOCATION FILE

Some buildings keep more than records.

Seiran Hospital is presented as a place of order: bright corridors, clinical routines and rooms designed to feel safe.

But familiarity can become uncomfortable when the smallest details stop matching what you remember. In The Girl in Seiran, the hospital is not simply a backdrop. Its spaces, documents, silence and repetition quietly participate in the story.

SUBJECT FILE // AOI

A girl carrying questions she cannot ignore.

Aoi stands at the center of the journey, but understanding her is not a matter of reading a simple profile.

Her experience is shaped by uncertainty, emotion and fragments that refuse to arrange themselves neatly. The player follows what she notices, what she fears and what she is willing to face — without being told which memory deserves to be trusted.

THEMATIC FILE // MEMORY

Memory is evidence. Memory is also unreliable.

The past does not arrive in Seiran as a clean timeline. It surfaces in pieces — sometimes protective, sometimes painful, sometimes incomplete.

The game uses memory as part of its psychological tension: not as a puzzle to solve instantly, but as something that changes the emotional meaning of a place as the player moves through it.
